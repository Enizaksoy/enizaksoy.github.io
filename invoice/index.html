Mills County Meat Invoice

Paste or photograph your notes, enter the prices, then send the invoice as a PDF. Amount = pounds × price per lb. Pieces are kept as a note, unless an item is priced each.

Table

Handwritten notes: copy the text with the iPhone first
  1. Open the photo in the Photos app.
  2. Tap the Live Text button (the square with lines, bottom right).
  3. Tap Copy All (or select the text and tap Copy).
  4. Come back here and tap 📋 Paste text.

The photo buttons read printed tables only. The iPhone reads handwriting much better.

Check and fix the text here. Write a group name (Small, Med) on its own line, then one pieces-pounds line per entry. Total lines are skipped automatically. Other items: Lamb fries = 133 pounds or Lamb heads = 120 (a count).

Prices

Enter the price for each item, per pound or each. Entries in the same group (all Smalls, all Meds) are added together and asked once. Prices are remembered on this phone.
