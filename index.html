AI / GPU Cluster Fabric

lossless RoCEv2 on Cisco Nexus 9332PQ

A 2-spine / 2-leaf Clos built on real hardware, not simulation — eBGP underlay, dual RDMA rails, and PFC/ECN/DCQCN tuned until the fabric stopped dropping under congestion. Raw bandwidth is capped by the 40G fabric, so the NIC generation shows up in collectives instead: NCCL all-reduce went from 3.66 to 6.08 GB/s. Every number was measured, then re-measured after tuning.

38.98 Gb/ssame-rail RDMA
~3 µssame-rail latency
96%drop reduction
+66%NCCL all-reduce

fabric  Cisco Nexus 9332PQ · 2-spine / 2-leaf Clos · eBGP underlay · ECMP · BFD
rdma    Mellanox ConnectX-5 Ex dual-port 100GbE · 40G link (switch-limited) · one leaf per rail · no LACP
lossless PFC · ECN marking · DCQCN · strict-priority queue
telemetry sFlow · Prometheus · Grafana · ERSPAN capture
